Reduced engine power Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Stop and check
Can you keep driving? Carefully, to a safe place or garage only. Power is limited.

What does the reduced engine power light mean?

The engine computer has limited engine power to protect the engine. It is often linked to the throttle, a sensor or the turbo.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Pull over safely, switch off and restart after a minute.
  2. Check for other warning lights.
  3. Read the fault codes with an OBD-II scanner.

When should I call a mechanic?

Get it diagnosed as soon as possible. If the car lurches or stalls, stop and call for help.

How much does it cost to fix?

A throttle body clean is about $75 to $150. A throttle body or sensor replacement is roughly $200 to $700. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
