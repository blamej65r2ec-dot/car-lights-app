P0172: System Too Rich (Bank 1)

Caution

What does P0172 mean?

The engine is getting too much fuel or too little air. The code is stored when the computer detects the problem, and it usually turns on the check engine light.

What causes P0172?

  • Leaking fuel injector
  • Dirty or faulty airflow sensor
  • Faulty fuel pressure regulator
  • Clogged air filter
  • Faulty oxygen sensor

How do I fix P0172?

  1. Replace a dirty air filter
  2. Clean the mass airflow sensor
  3. Check fuel pressure and the regulator
  4. Test the injectors

Can I keep driving with P0172?

Usually yes for a short time, but get it checked soon. Stop if the car shakes, loses power or the check engine light flashes.

How much does it cost to fix?

An air filter is $15 to $40. An injector or regulator repair is roughly $150 to $600. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
