P0113: Intake Air Temperature Sensor Circuit High

Caution
Can you keep driving? Usually yes for a short time, but get it checked soon. Stop if the car shakes, loses power or the check engine light flashes.

What does P0113 mean?

The intake air temperature sensor is reporting an unusually low temperature signal. The code is stored when the computer detects the problem, and it usually turns on the check engine light.

What causes P0113?

  • Faulty sensor
  • Open circuit or damaged wiring
  • Loose connector

How do I fix P0113?

  1. Inspect the connector and wiring
  2. Replace the sensor

How much does it cost to fix?

A sensor is roughly $50 to $200 fitted. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
