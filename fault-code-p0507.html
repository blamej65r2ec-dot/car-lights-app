P0507: Idle Control System RPM Higher Than Expected

Caution

What does P0507 mean?

The engine is idling faster than it should. The code is stored when the computer detects the problem, and it usually turns on the check engine light.

What causes P0507?

  • Vacuum leak
  • Dirty throttle body
  • Faulty idle air control valve

How do I fix P0507?

  1. Check for vacuum leaks
  2. Clean the throttle body
  3. Replace the idle air control valve

Can I keep driving with P0507?

Usually yes for a short time, but get it checked soon. Stop if the car shakes, loses power or the check engine light flashes.

How much does it cost to fix?

A throttle body clean is about $75 to $150. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
