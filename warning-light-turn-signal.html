Turn signal Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Information

What does the turn signal light mean?

Normal while signaling. If it blinks much faster than usual, a bulb has failed.

Can I keep driving with it on?

Yes, but fix it soon. Other drivers cannot see your signal.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Find which bulb is out and replace it (see the manual).

When should I call a mechanic?

If all bulbs are fine, a flasher relay may be faulty. It is a quick repair.

How much does it cost to fix?

Bulbs are about $5 to $20 each. A flasher relay is roughly $10 to $60. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
