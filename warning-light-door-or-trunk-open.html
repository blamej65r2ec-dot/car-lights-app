Door or trunk open Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Stop and check

What does the door or trunk open light mean?

A door, trunk or hood that is not fully latched, or a dirty or faulty latch sensor.

Can I keep driving with it on?

Not until everything is closed.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Check every door, the trunk and the hood, and close them firmly.
  2. If the light stays on, clean the latch with a rag and add a little lubricant.

When should I call a mechanic?

If everything is closed and the light stays on, a latch sensor is probably faulty. It is a minor repair.

How much does it cost to fix?

Usually free to $100 for a latch cleaning or a new sensor. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
