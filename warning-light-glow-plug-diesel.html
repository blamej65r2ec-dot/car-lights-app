Glow plug (diesel) Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Caution
Can you keep driving? Yes, but expect hard starting.

What does the glow plug (diesel) light mean?

Normal: the engine is pre-heating before a cold start. If it stays on or appears while driving: a faulty glow plug, relay or engine sensor.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Wait for the light to go out before starting.
  2. If it stays on, switch off and restart once.

When should I call a mechanic?

A scan can identify the fault. Hard starts and white smoke are signs of failing glow plugs.

How much does it cost to fix?

Glow plugs are about $20 to $60 each, roughly $200 to $600 for a full set with labor. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
