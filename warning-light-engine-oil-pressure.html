Engine oil pressure Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Stop and check

What does the engine oil pressure light mean?

Low oil level, an oil leak, a worn oil pump, a clogged filter, a faulty pressure sensor, or the wrong oil grade.

Can I keep driving with it on?

No. Stop at once. Driving even a few minutes can destroy the engine.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Park on level ground and switch off.
  2. Wait 5 minutes, then check the dipstick.
  3. If the level is low, add the oil grade listed in your manual.
  4. If the light goes out and stays out, drive gently to a garage to check for a leak.

When should I call a mechanic?

If the oil level is fine but the light stays on, or you hear knocking, do not start the engine. Call a tow. A faulty sensor is possible, but only a mechanic can confirm it.

How much does it cost to fix?

From $10 for oil to about $250 for a pressure sensor. An oil pump or engine repair can cost $1,000 or more. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
