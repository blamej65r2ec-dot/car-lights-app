Power steering Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Stop and check

What does the power steering light mean?

The power steering assist has failed or is reduced. Causes include low fluid on hydraulic systems, a failing pump, a blown fuse, or a fault in the electric steering motor or sensors.

Can I keep driving with it on?

Not for long. The car still steers but needs much more effort, so stop safely and get it checked.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Park safely and restart the car.
  2. On hydraulic systems, check the power steering fluid level.
  3. Check the power steering fuse (see the manual).
  4. Avoid tight, slow-speed turns until it is checked.

When should I call a mechanic?

If the light stays on, or you hear whining or groaning, have it inspected. Electric steering faults need a scan tool.

How much does it cost to fix?

Fluid is about $10 to $20. A steering pump is roughly $300 to $900 fitted. Electric steering repairs can cost $500 to $2,000. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
