Low fuel Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Caution

What does the low fuel light mean?

About 10% of the tank is left, usually 30 to 60 miles (50 to 100 km) of range.

Can I keep driving with it on?

Yes, to a fuel station now.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Refuel soon.
  2. Avoid running the tank dry. It can damage the fuel pump and clog the filter.

When should I call a mechanic?

If the light stays on after refueling, the fuel level sender may be faulty.

How much does it cost to fix?

The price of a tank of fuel. A failed fuel level sender is roughly $150 to $400. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
