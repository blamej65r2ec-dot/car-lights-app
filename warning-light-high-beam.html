High beam Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Information

What does the high beam light mean?

The high beams are on.

Can I keep driving with it on?

Yes.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Push or pull the stalk to switch to low beam.

When should I call a mechanic?

If the beams will not switch off, check the stalk and the fuse.

How much does it cost to fix?

Free. If the beams will not turn off, a switch repair is about $50 to $200. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
