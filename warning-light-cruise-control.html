Cruise control Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Information
Can you keep driving? Yes.

What does the cruise control light mean?

Cruise control is switched on or a speed is set.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Tap the brake or press the cancel or off button.

When should I call a mechanic?

If it will not engage, a faulty brake light switch is a common cause.

How much does it cost to fix?

A brake light switch is roughly $15 to $60, plus labor if a shop does it. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
