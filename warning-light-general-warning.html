General warning Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Caution

What does the general warning light mean?

The car wants to show a message. It could be a low fluid, an open door, a service reminder or something else.

Can I keep driving with it on?

Depends on the message.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Read the message on the instrument display.
  2. Look up the exact meaning in your manual.

When should I call a mechanic?

If the message mentions the engine, brakes or steering, treat it as serious.

How much does it cost to fix?

Depends on the message. Many are free to fix. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
