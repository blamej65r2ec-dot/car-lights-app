Battery or charging Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Stop and check

What does the battery or charging light mean?

A failing alternator, a loose or broken belt, corroded battery terminals, or a weak battery.

Can I keep driving with it on?

Short distance only. The car may stall once the battery drains.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Switch off the A/C, radio, heated seats and other extras.
  2. Check that the belt at the front of the engine is intact, if you can see it.
  3. With the engine off, clean corroded terminals and make sure they are tight.
  4. Head straight to a garage or battery shop.

When should I call a mechanic?

A mechanic can test the alternator and battery in minutes. Do not wait: once the battery is flat, the car will not restart.

How much does it cost to fix?

A battery is about $120 to $300. An alternator is roughly $400 to $900 fitted. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
