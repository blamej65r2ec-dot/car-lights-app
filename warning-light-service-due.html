Service due Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Caution

What does the service due light mean?

The car reached its service interval by distance or time.

Can I keep driving with it on?

Yes.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Book a service.
  2. After the service, the light is reset. Many cars let you reset it from the dashboard menu (see the manual).

When should I call a mechanic?

Skipping services shortens engine life and can affect your warranty.

How much does it cost to fix?

A basic service is about $50 to $150. A major service is roughly $200 to $600. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
