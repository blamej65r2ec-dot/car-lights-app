P0562: System Voltage Low

Caution

What does P0562 mean?

The electrical system voltage is lower than it should be. The code is stored when the computer detects the problem, and it usually turns on the check engine light.

What causes P0562?

  • Weak battery
  • Failing alternator
  • Loose or corroded connections

How do I fix P0562?

  1. Test the battery and alternator
  2. Clean and tighten the terminals

Can I keep driving with P0562?

Usually yes for a short time, but get it checked soon. Stop if the car shakes, loses power or the check engine light flashes.

How much does it cost to fix?

A battery is about $120 to $300. An alternator is roughly $400 to $900 fitted. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
