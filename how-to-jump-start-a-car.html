How to Jump-Start a Car Safely

Wear eye protection. Do not jump a battery that is cracked, leaking or frozen.

With jumper cables

  1. Park the working car close, without the cars touching, and switch both off.
  2. Connect one red clamp to the positive (+) terminal of the dead battery.
  3. Connect the other red clamp to the positive (+) terminal of the good battery.
  4. Connect one black clamp to the negative (−) terminal of the good battery.
  5. Connect the last black clamp to bare, unpainted metal on the dead car’s engine, away from the battery.
  6. Start the working car and wait a few minutes, then start the dead car.
  7. Remove the clamps in the reverse order.
  8. Drive for 20 to 30 minutes to recharge the battery.

With a portable jump starter

Follow the same positive and negative connections, using the starter’s own instructions. You do not need a second car.
