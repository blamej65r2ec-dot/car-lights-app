P0303: Cylinder 3 Misfire Detected

Caution

What does P0303 mean?

The engine is misfiring in cylinder 3. The code is stored when the computer detects the problem, and it usually turns on the check engine light.

What causes P0303?

  • A worn spark plug or failing ignition coil on cylinder 3
  • A clogged or leaking fuel injector
  • Low compression
  • A vacuum leak near the cylinder

How do I fix P0303?

  1. Swap the ignition coil with another cylinder and see if the code follows
  2. Replace the spark plug
  3. Check the injector
  4. If the check engine light flashes, stop driving

Can I keep driving with P0303?

Usually yes for a short time, but get it checked soon. Stop if the car shakes, loses power or the check engine light flashes.

How much does it cost to fix?

A spark plug and coil repair is typically $100 to $400. Engine repairs cost much more. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
