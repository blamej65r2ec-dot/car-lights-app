Transmission Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Stop and check

What does the transmission light mean?

Low or old transmission fluid, overheating from towing or heavy traffic, or a solenoid or sensor fault.

Can I keep driving with it on?

Slow down and stop soon.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Pull over safely and let the car rest for 30 minutes.
  2. If your car has a transmission dipstick, check the fluid (follow the manual).
  3. Avoid towing and hard acceleration until it is checked.

When should I call a mechanic?

Slipping, jerking, a burning smell, or being stuck in one gear needs a mechanic. Do not keep driving: repairs get expensive quickly.

How much does it cost to fix?

A fluid change is about $100 to $300. Solenoid or valve body repairs run $500 to $2,000, and a rebuild can cost $2,500 or more. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
