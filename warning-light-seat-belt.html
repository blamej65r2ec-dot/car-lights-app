Seat belt Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Stop and check

What does the seat belt light mean?

A front seat belt is not buckled, or the seat sensor or buckle switch is faulty.

Can I keep driving with it on?

Yes, once the belts are fastened.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Fasten every seat belt, including the passenger belt.
  2. Remove heavy items from the passenger seat, which can trigger the sensor.
  3. Clean the buckle and make sure it clicks fully.

When should I call a mechanic?

If the light stays on with all belts fastened, the buckle switch or seat sensor may be faulty and should be checked.

How much does it cost to fix?

Often free. A buckle switch or seat sensor repair is roughly $100 to $400. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
