How to Check and Set Your Tire Pressure

It takes five minutes and helps with safety, fuel use and tire life.

  1. Find the recommended pressure on the sticker inside the driver’s door or in the owner’s manual. Do not use the maximum number printed on the tire.
  2. Check when the tires are cold: before driving, or after the car has been parked for a few hours.
  3. Remove the valve cap, press the gauge on firmly and read the number.
  4. Add air, or press the valve pin to let some out, until it matches the recommended pressure.
  5. Check all four tires, and the spare if you have one, then refit the caps.

Some cars need the tire pressure warning light reset after inflating. See the manual. More about the tire pressure light.
