Diesel particulate filter Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Caution

What does the diesel particulate filter light mean?

Too many short trips, so the filter cannot burn off the soot it collects.

Can I keep driving with it on?

Yes, but act soon.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Drive 20 minutes or more at a steady speed (about 60 mph or 100 km/h) on a highway.
  2. Use good quality diesel and the correct low-ash engine oil.

When should I call a mechanic?

If the light stays on or the car loses power, a garage can force a cleaning. Ignoring it can ruin the filter, which is expensive.

How much does it cost to fix?

A forced clean is about $100 to $300. A new filter can cost $1,000 to $3,000. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
