P0455: Evaporative Emission System Leak Detected (Large Leak)

Caution
Can you keep driving? Usually yes for a short time, but get it checked soon. Stop if the car shakes, loses power or the check engine light flashes.

What does P0455 mean?

The fuel vapor system has a large leak. The code is stored when the computer detects the problem, and it usually turns on the check engine light.

What causes P0455?

  • Loose or missing fuel cap
  • Cracked EVAP hose
  • Faulty purge or vent valve
  • Damaged charcoal canister

How do I fix P0455?

  1. Tighten or replace the fuel cap and clear the code
  2. Inspect the EVAP hoses
  3. Replace the purge or vent valve

How much does it cost to fix?

A fuel cap is $15 to $30. A purge valve is roughly $100 to $300 fitted. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
