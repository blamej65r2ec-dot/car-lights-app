Engine temperature Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Stop and check

What does the engine temperature light mean?

Low coolant, a leak, a stuck thermostat, a failed water pump or fan, a blocked radiator, or a broken belt.

Can I keep driving with it on?

No. Stop safely and switch off the engine.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Pull over and switch off.
  2. Wait at least 30 minutes. Never open the radiator cap while the engine is hot.
  3. When cold, check the coolant level and top up with the correct coolant.
  4. Look under the car for drips.

When should I call a mechanic?

If it overheats again, steam comes out, or coolant keeps disappearing, call a tow. Driving an overheated engine can warp the cylinder head.

How much does it cost to fix?

A thermostat or coolant top-up is about $20 to $250. A water pump or radiator is roughly $300 to $900. Head gasket repair can exceed $1,500. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
