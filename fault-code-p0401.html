P0401: Exhaust Gas Recirculation Flow Insufficient

Caution

What does P0401 mean?

Not enough exhaust gas is flowing through the EGR system. The code is stored when the computer detects the problem, and it usually turns on the check engine light.

What causes P0401?

  • Clogged EGR passages
  • Stuck EGR valve
  • Faulty EGR sensor

How do I fix P0401?

  1. Clean the EGR valve and passages
  2. Test the EGR valve
  3. Replace the sensor or valve if needed

Can I keep driving with P0401?

Usually yes for a short time, but get it checked soon. Stop if the car shakes, loses power or the check engine light flashes.

How much does it cost to fix?

Cleaning is about $100 to $250. An EGR valve is roughly $200 to $600 fitted. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
