AdBlue or DEF Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Caution

What does the adblue or def light mean?

The diesel exhaust fluid is low or the system has a fault. Some cars will not restart once it runs out.

Can I keep driving with it on?

Yes, but refill soon. Many cars will not restart after the fluid is empty.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Refill with AdBlue or DEF (never water) at the blue cap, usually next to the fuel filler or in the trunk.
  2. Do not wait for the countdown to finish.

When should I call a mechanic?

If the light stays on after refilling, a sensor, heater or pump fault is likely and needs a scan.

How much does it cost to fix?

AdBlue is about $15 to $30 for a jug. A sensor or pump repair is roughly $300 to $1,200. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
