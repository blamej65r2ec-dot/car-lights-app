Check engine Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Caution

What does the check engine light mean?

A loose fuel cap, a faulty oxygen sensor, worn spark plugs or ignition coil, a failing catalytic converter, or many other engine and emissions faults.

Can I keep driving with it on?

Steady: yes, carefully. Flashing: no.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Tighten the fuel cap and drive for a few days to see whether the light clears.
  2. Read the fault code with an OBD-II scanner. They are cheap, and many auto parts stores do it for free.
  3. Search the code (for example P0420) to learn what it points to.

When should I call a mechanic?

If the light flashes, the car shakes, or power drops, stop driving and see a mechanic. A flashing light can damage the catalytic converter.

How much does it cost to fix?

A fuel cap is $15 to $30. An oxygen sensor is about $150 to $400. A catalytic converter can cost $1,000 to $2,500. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
