ABS Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Caution

What does the abs light mean?

A dirty or damaged wheel speed sensor, low brake fluid, a blown fuse, or a faulty ABS module.

Can I keep driving with it on?

Yes, with extra care. Normal braking still works.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Check the brake fluid level.
  2. Check the ABS fuse (see the manual).
  3. Restart the car.

When should I call a mechanic?

A scan tool shows which wheel sensor failed. Sensors are a common and fairly cheap fix.

How much does it cost to fix?

A wheel speed sensor is roughly $100 to $300 fitted. An ABS module can cost $500 to $1,500. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
