Airbag Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Stop and check
Can you keep driving? Yes, but the airbags may not work in a crash.

What does the airbag light mean?

A faulty seat sensor, a loose connector under the seat, a crash sensor fault, or a damaged clock spring in the steering wheel.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Remove heavy objects and thick seat covers from the passenger seat.
  2. Restart the car to see whether the light clears.
  3. Do not try to repair airbag parts yourself.

When should I call a mechanic?

Airbag repair must be done by a professional. Book it soon: this light means reduced crash protection.

How much does it cost to fix?

Diagnosis is about $100 to $150. A seat sensor or clock spring is roughly $200 to $700. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
