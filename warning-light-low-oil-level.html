Low oil level Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Caution
Can you keep driving? Yes for a short distance, but top up the oil as soon as you can.

What does the low oil level light mean?

The engine oil level is low. Some cars use a level sensor, and this light is different from the oil pressure light.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Park on level ground and switch off.
  2. Wait 5 minutes, then check the dipstick.
  3. Add the oil grade listed in the manual until it reaches the full mark.

When should I call a mechanic?

If you need to add oil often, the engine may be burning or leaking oil. Have it checked.

How much does it cost to fix?

Oil is about $10 to $40 to top up. Fixing a leak is roughly $150 to $1,000 or more. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
