P0700: Transmission Control System Malfunction

Caution

What does P0700 mean?

The transmission computer has stored a fault and asked the engine computer to turn on the light. The code is stored when the computer detects the problem, and it usually turns on the check engine light.

What causes P0700?

  • Any transmission fault
  • Low or old fluid
  • Faulty sensor or solenoid

How do I fix P0700?

  1. Read the transmission-specific codes with a scanner
  2. Check the fluid level and condition

Can I keep driving with P0700?

Usually yes for a short time, but get it checked soon. Stop if the car shakes, loses power or the check engine light flashes.

How much does it cost to fix?

Depends on the underlying fault. A fluid change is about $100 to $300. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
