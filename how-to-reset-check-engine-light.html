How to Reset the Check Engine Light

Fix the cause first. Clearing the light only hides the problem.

1. Find out why it is on

Plug in an OBD-II scanner and read the code. Here is how to read it, and our fault code pages explain the usual causes. A loose fuel cap is a common one.

2. Fix the problem

Often the light goes out on its own after the repair, once the car has finished several normal drives.

3. Clear the code with a scanner

  1. Turn the ignition on with the engine off.
  2. Choose the clear or erase codes option on the scanner.
  3. Start the car and check that the light stays off.

What not to do

Do not clear a code just to hide a fault. Disconnecting the battery is not a good method: it can erase radio and other settings, and the car may need several drives before it passes an emissions test.

If the light comes back

The fault is still there. Read the code again, or see a mechanic. More about the check engine light.
