Stability control Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Caution

What does the stability control light mean?

Flashing: the system is working on a slippery road. Steady: it was switched off, or a wheel speed, steering angle or ABS sensor has a fault.

Can I keep driving with it on?

Yes, carefully.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Check that the ESP OFF button was not pressed.
  2. Turn the steering wheel fully left and right, then restart the car.
  3. Check the brake fluid level.

When should I call a mechanic?

If it stays on, have the sensors scanned. Faults here are often linked to ABS.

How much does it cost to fix?

Sensor faults typically cost $100 to $400. Module repairs can cost more. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
