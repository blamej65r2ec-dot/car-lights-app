Immobilizer or key Warning Light: What It Means and What to Do

Caution

What does the immobilizer or key light mean?

A weak key battery, a damaged key chip, or a fault in the car’s antenna or computer.

Can I keep driving with it on?

If it starts, yes. If it does not, you cannot drive.

How can I fix it myself?

  1. Try your spare key.
  2. Replace the key fob battery.
  3. Keep other keys and fobs away from the ignition.

When should I call a mechanic?

If neither key works, call the dealer or an auto locksmith. A tow is often needed.

How much does it cost to fix?

A key fob battery is about $5. A new programmed key is roughly $100 to $400. These are rough US ranges and vary by car and shop.
