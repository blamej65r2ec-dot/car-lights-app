What Do Dashboard Warning Light Colors Mean?

The color tells you how urgent the problem is.

Red: stop and check

A red light means a serious problem or a safety issue. Stop safely as soon as you can and check the car before driving on.

Yellow or orange: caution

Yellow or orange means something needs attention soon. You can usually keep driving for a short time, but get it checked.

Green or blue: information

These lights show that a feature is on, such as high beams or cruise control. No action is needed.

Flashing or steady?

A flashing light is usually more urgent than a steady one. A flashing check engine light, for example, means stop driving.
